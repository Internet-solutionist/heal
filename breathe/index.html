A short reset

A minute of breathing

The circle grows while you breathe in, rests while you hold, and shrinks while you breathe out. Four seconds in, four seconds hold, six seconds out. Tap the circle to start. Tap again to pause.

If holding your breath feels wrong, stop. You can simply breathe out a little longer than you breathe in, with no hold.

Ready. Breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 4, then breathe out for 6.

  1. Breathe in 4s
  2. Hold 4s
  3. Breathe out 6s

This is a calming exercise, not a treatment. Stop if you feel dizzy or unwell. It does not replace care from a clinic.

If things feel heavy, talk to someone you trust, or SADAG on 0800 567 567.
